Research source ledger for HUD test bench survey

Primary vendor sources
- EVERFINE HUD-3000: https://www.everfine.cn/pro_desc_440.html
  Public parameters: VID 2–15 m; FOV 22°×16°; system movement resolution 0.01 mm and accuracy ±0.1 mm; rotation resolution 0.01° and accuracy ±0.1°; luminance accuracy ±3%; range 0.001–15000 cd/m²; luminance repeatability ±0.1%; chromaticity accuracy ±0.003; chromaticity repeatability 0.0002.
- YANDING RT-HUD V3: https://yanding.com/product/detail?id=1960
  Public parameters: windshield rotation 25°–70°; X ±250 mm, Y ±750 mm, Z ±80 mm. Page does not publish axis accuracy/resolution.
- Radiant Vision Systems TT-HUD: https://www.radiantvisionsystems.com/zh-hans/node/174
  Public positioning: a ProMetric imaging photometer/colorimeter plus TT-HUD software; camera 16 MP or higher. Product page does not present an integrated XYZ gantry or motion-axis specifications.
- Shanghai FullLight FLTS-HUD: https://www.fulllightcn.com/product-item-102.html
  Public positioning: automated optical/electrical test system with high-precision servo control and real-time feedback; no numeric axis travel/accuracy disclosed.
- ColorSpace HUD solution: https://colorspace.com.cn/cs/cn/product/hud-display-solution.html
  Public positioning: 6-axis motion platform, windshield-angle mechanism, sunlight simulator, automated analysis/reporting; no numeric axis travel/accuracy disclosed.

Additional corroboration
- Shanghai University procurement notice: https://bidding.shu.edu.cn/sy/zbkscg_detail_phone.jsp?fl=A&gglx=KCGG&lx=KCGG&wid=202211050023837056
  Movement requirement: resolution ≤0.02 mm, accuracy ≤±0.1 mm; rotation resolution ≤0.02°, accuracy ≤±0.1°. This is a buyer requirement, not a confirmed vendor product specification.
- Konica Minolta HUD quality solution: https://www.konicaminolta.com.cn/instruments/solution/automotive/hud/index.html
  Explains HUD measurements and use of ProMetric/TT-HUD under SAE J1757-2 and ISO 15008.

Method note
- Research cut-off: 2026-07-26.
- “未公开” means the reviewed public vendor page did not disclose a numeric value; it does not mean the product cannot meet a requirement.
- Axis naming is retained as published by each vendor. Direction conventions must be confirmed against mechanical drawings before procurement.
